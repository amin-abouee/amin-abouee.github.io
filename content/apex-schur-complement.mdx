A comprehensive refactoring pass on APEX Solver landed this month.

Both Schur complement paths were reworked and benchmarked — the implicit solver (preconditioned conjugate gradient) and the explicit solver (sparse Cholesky) — eliminating redundant rewrites in the reduction and back-substitution stages that SLAM-style problems spend most of their time in.

The crate is also now fully `clippy::pedantic`-clean, which keeps the factor-graph internals honest as the API grows.

For the details, see the repository: [aabouee/apex-solver](https://github.com/aabouee/apex-solver).
